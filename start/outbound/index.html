出了 BC，MSP 还管多少

MSP 在国外只补一小部分在加拿大其他省离开多久，MSP 还在出境险先看的条款

MSP 在国外只补一小部分

境外住院，MSP 每天最多付 75 加元，超出部分自付。

原文与来源
Payment for hospital services (eg, in-patient medical care, facility fees, etc) is limited to $75.00 Canadian per diem.
查看官方文件 ↗核对：2026-10-03

境外医生费按同样服务在 BC 的收费标准付，差额自付。

原文与来源
Payment for physician services (eg, consultations, surgeon fees, etc) will not exceed the amount payable had the same services been performed in B.C.
查看官方文件 ↗核对：2026-10-03

省外的处方药、医疗用品和救护车，省里都不付。

原文与来源
PharmaCare does not provide coverage for prescription drugs or medical supplies when obtained outside B.C. Provincial assistance is also not provided to subsidize payment for ambulance services outside B.C.
查看官方文件 ↗核对：2026-10-03

省外的执业护士、医生助理、脊医、物理治疗师等非医生治疗，一律不保。

原文与来源
MSP does not provide any coverage for treatment provided by a health care practitioner outside the province (e.g. physician assistants, nurse practitioners, chiropractors or physical therapists).
查看官方文件 ↗核对：2026-10-03

官方建议：哪怕只出国一天，也另买私人医疗险，补住院医生差额、药、救护车和送回 BC 的费用。

原文与来源
For complete protection, additional medical insurance should be purchased from a private insurance company, even if you only plan to leave the country for a day.
查看官方文件 ↗核对：2026-10-03

在加拿大其他省

魁北克以外的省份，出示有效的 BC Services Card，多数医生会直接向他们本省医保收费。

原文与来源
Most physicians in other Canadian provinces and territories (except Quebec) will bill their own provincial/territorial health plan for services provided if you present your valid B.C. Services Card.
查看官方文件 ↗核对：2026-10-03

在魁北克就诊可能要自己先付再报销；药和救护车在省外同样不由 BC 承担。

离开多久，MSP 还在

只为度假离开 BC 的居民，一个日历年内累计最多可离开 7 个月。

原文与来源
Eligible B.C. residents ... who are outside B.C. for vacation purposes only, are allowed a total absence of up to seven months in a calendar year
查看官方文件 ↗核对：2026-10-03

离开 6 个月或以上要先联系 Health Insurance BC；符合条件的，5 年内可有一次最长 24 个月的延长离境。

原文与来源
Residents who will be absent from B.C. for six months or more in a calendar year, need to contact Health Insurance BC … individuals may retain eligibility for coverage during an extended absence of up to 24 consecutive months, once in a 60 month (five year) period.
查看官方文件 ↗核对：2026-10-03

专程出境看非急诊病须事先审批，否则全额自付；旅费食宿不付。

原文与来源
Provincial coverage for elective medical care outside of Canada (even if the medical care is urgently required) requires pre-approval.
查看官方文件 ↗核对：2026-10-03

超出离境上限会失去 MSP，回来后按「重新建立居所」再走等待期。这是按官方规则推出的后果，没有哪一页直接这样写。

出境险先看的条款

既往症回看期按年龄分：PBC 对 60 岁及以下看出发前 3 个月，61 岁及以上看 6 个月。各家定义不同，要读自己那张保单。

原文与来源
we will evaluate pre-existing conditions for people 60 years and younger based on their health within the previous 3 months prior to the day their coverage begins. For people age 61 and over, we evaluate based on their health within 6 months prior.
查看官方文件 ↗核对：2026-10-03

有些出境险要求你持有有效的 BC Care Card 才能买。

原文与来源
In order for you to qualify to purchase travel insurance, you must have a valid Care Card number.
查看官方文件 ↗核对：2026-10-03

理赔要先报 MSP 再报私人保险，除非该保险公司与卫生厅另有协议。

原文与来源
physician and hospital claims must be submitted to Health Insurance BC before they are sent to a private insurance company
查看官方文件 ↗核对：2026-10-03

医生费 90 天内、住院费出院后 6 个月内向 MSP 报。

原文与来源
Submit claims for physician services within 90 days of the date of service. In-patient hospital claims (and any associated medical claims) must be submitted within six months of discharge.
查看官方文件 ↗核对：2026-10-03
